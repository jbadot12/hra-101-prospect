A brief introduction to Unlock HRAs

From a consumer question to an appropriate next step.

A Health Risk Assessment (HRA) is a clinically grounded, pre-diagnostic digital experience that typically takes less than five minutes. It gives consumers useful education and recommended next steps while creating consented first-party insight for the healthcare organization.

FastTypically completed in under five minutes
RelevantEducation and a recommended next step
ConnectedFirst-party insight that supports action
Client-branded experiences designed to feel like a natural part of the consumer journey.

01 · Why HRAs matter

Meet consumers before they are ready to schedule.

A health question often appears before a person has chosen a provider—or even decided they need care. An HRA gives that person something useful to do now and gives the health system a relevant way to continue the relationship.

For the consumer

Education that reduces uncertainty.

A short, approachable experience helps someone make sense of a concern without asking them to diagnose themselves.

  • Learn about risk factors, symptoms, or screening needs
  • Receive a personalized, easy-to-understand result
  • See a recommended next step that matches the result
For the healthcare organization

First-party insight that can inspire action.

Consent-based responses help teams understand consumer needs, segment interest, and connect the right audiences to supported care pathways.

  • Identify and segment consumers by assessment outcome
  • Recommend targeted calls to action and care encounters
  • Measure engagement from entry source through downstream outcomes

Clinical foundation

Clinically grounded

Assessment-specific questions and result logic use recognized models, validated instruments, guidelines, and other condition-specific sources where applicable.

Consumer experience

White-label by design

Brand, imagery, messaging, domain, and calls to action can align with the healthcare organization’s digital experience.

Program strategy

Configured around the strategy

Custom questions, pathways, selection pages, and calls to action connect the experience to service-line goals and available care.

02 · How HRAs fit into strategy

The HRA is the conversion point—not the entire campaign.

The strongest programs place a relevant HRA wherever consumers already encounter the brand. Paid channels create and capture demand; owned channels turn existing attention into action.

✦
One assessment can support many entry points.

The message and destination should match consumer intent, but each path can connect to the same clinically grounded experience.

A subscription is not priced by completion, enabling broad activation across relevant touchpoints.

Paid search · Capture active hand raisers

Match a specific search to a focused HRA path.

Search reaches consumers using terms that signal they are primed to learn or act. A targeted landing page provides context before inviting them into the HRA.

Role in the journey: Capture high intent while the consumer is actively looking for answers or care.
