01 · Establish the value

A small step for consumers. A strategic signal for health systems.

Consumers are not always ready to schedule care the moment a question appears. An HRA gives them something useful to do now while creating a measurable path toward an appropriate next step.

For the consumer

Education that reduces uncertainty.

A short, approachable experience helps someone make sense of a concern without asking them to diagnose themselves.

  • Learn about risk factors, symptoms, or screening needs
  • Receive a personalized, easy-to-understand result
  • See a recommended next step that matches the result
For the healthcare organization

First-party insight that can inspire action.

Consent-based responses help teams understand consumer needs, segment interest, and connect the right audiences to supported care pathways.

  • Identify and segment consumers by assessment outcome
  • Recommend targeted calls to action and care encounters
  • Measure engagement from entry source through downstream outcomes

02 · Build the activation

The HRA is the conversion layer—not the entire strategy.

The strongest programs place a relevant HRA wherever consumers already encounter the brand. Paid channels create and capture demand; owned channels turn existing attention into action.

✦
One assessment can support many entry points.

The message and destination should match consumer intent, but each path can connect to the same clinically grounded experience.

A subscription is not priced by completion, enabling broad activation across relevant touchpoints.

Paid search · Capture active hand raisers

Search reaches consumers using terms that signal they are primed to learn or act. A targeted landing page provides context before inviting them into the HRA.

Role in the journey: Capture high intent while the consumer is actively looking for answers or care.

04 · See the connected experience

The questions matter. So does everything around them.

A useful HRA journey begins before the first question and continues after the result. Explore the stages to see how strategy, clinical grounding, brand, and calls to action work together.

Reach · Create a relevant door

Meet a consumer in the moment of curiosity.

Search, social, email, a website, a QR code, and other campaign touchpoints can all lead to an HRA. The message should match the consumer’s intent.

Design principle: An HRA should feel like a useful next step—not simply another landing page.

Clinically grounded

Assessment-specific questions and result logic use recognized models, validated instruments, guidelines, and other condition-specific sources where applicable.

White-label by design

Brand, imagery, messaging, domain, and calls to action can align with the healthcare organization’s digital experience.

Configured around the strategy

Custom questions, pathways, selection pages, and calls to action connect the experience to real service-line goals and available care.

05 · Continue and learn

HRAs do not stop on the results screen.

Personalized actions, follow-up, connected systems, and reporting help turn a short consumer interaction into a measurable program that can improve over time.

Recommend the next right action

Match the result to a supported pathway.

Different outcomes can lead to different calls to action based on clinical relevance, service-line capacity, and operational ownership.

  • Scheduling, callbacks, and provider search
  • Screening, education, locations, or related assessments
  • Custom messages and actions by result category

Continue the relationship

Build trust through relevant follow-up.

Teams can use follow-up built into HRA Management, connect existing CRM and marketing automation systems, or combine both approaches.

  • Email the consumer’s HRA report
  • Send nurture and reminder communications
  • Extend consented data into approved workflows

Manage, measure, improve

See the journey from engagement to action.

Reporting can surface starts, completions, results, traffic sources, demographics, CTA performance, trends, and—when approved attribution is available—downstream encounters and business outcomes.
